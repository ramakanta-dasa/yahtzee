How to play 🎲

Roll all five dice. Tap a die to hold it. Roll again to reroll only dice that are not held. You can change which dice are held between rolls.

Each Yahtzee turn allows up to three rolls, including the first. When you have used all three, start a new turn. This app rolls dice only, so keep your scorecard separately.
